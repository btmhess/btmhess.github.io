requirements: |
  Decorative lamp with full internet control, rgb coloring, and normal white light. Inspired by a lamp seen at costco for $100.

result: |
  I built two black lamps with polyethylene tube diffusers and two white lamps with printed diffusers.<br>
  The four lamps are currently in use daily, with automated scene lighting control. Cost per lamp comes out to $77.50 each, beating the example lamp by 25% with much better functionality.

specifications: |
  Decorative lamp featuring three entwined spiral arms.<br>
  Each arm contains 87 WRGB (White Red Green Blue) LED elements.<br>
  Lights are diffused through either clear polyethylene tubing or matte-white 3d-printed structure.<br>
  White lamps are printed entirely in "vase mode", resulting in a 0.4mm thick continuous wall with no seams or layers.<br>
  Each lamp base contains the controlling ESP-32 and a 5v20a power supply.<br>
  Custom programmable patterns; "rainbow" and "full-white" shown in gallery.<br>
  Power consumption: 30w at full natural-white phosphor only, 90w at full WRGB activation.<br>
