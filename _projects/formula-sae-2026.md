specifications: |
  **Dimensions & Weight**<br>
  Weight (without driver, with tractive battery): 238.1 kg<br>
  Weight distribution: 46.3% front, 50.0% left<br>
  Overall Dimensions: 2954 mm (L) x 1499 mm (W) x 1189 mm (H)<br>
  Wheelbase: 1575 mm<br><br>

  **Powertrain & Battery**<br>
  Motor: EMRAX 228 (LV +43%)<br>
  Motor Controller: Drivetrain Innovations HV-850<br>
  Peak Power: 80 kW<br>
  Tractive System Voltage: 403.2 V max / 355.2 V nominal<br>
  Tractive Battery Weight: 47.2 kg<br>
  Battery Pack: 96S1P configuration, 96 total cells, 6 modules<br>
  Battery Cells: Melasta SLPBB075175 LiCoO2 pouch cells (17.5 Ah capacity)<br>
  Battery Management System: Thunderstruck Master Control Unit and Satellites<br><br>

  **Drivetrain & Chassis**<br>
  Drive Type: 1-speed chain drive (11-tooth drive sprocket, 60-tooth rear driven sprocket)<br>
  Final Drive Reduction Ratio: 5.45:1<br>
  Frame Construction: Welded 4130 Chromoly Steel Space Frame (GTAW TIG-welded)<br>
  Torsional Stiffness: 2224.76 N-m/deg (physical test)<br>
  Suspension: Independent Double-Wishbone (SLA) with push rod actuated shock via rocker<br>
  Tires: Hoosier Racing Tire R20 Compound (18.1" Diameter, 6.2" Treadwidth)<br>
  Wheels: Magnesium Alloy, 1-piece cast (10.0" diameter, 7.0" width)<br><br>

  **Aerodynamics & Bodywork**<br>
  Configuration: Wing-dominant aero configuration with venturi effect floor<br>
  Forces (at 80 kph): 805.84 N Downforce (57.2% front balance), 371.25 N Drag<br>
  Coefficients: Cl: -2.55, Cd: 1.18<br>
  Notable Features: Adjustable rear wing for event-specific tuning<br>
  Materials: Carbon fiber (wings, nose cone, undertray, diffuser) and fiberglass (side panels)<br><br>

  Top Speed: 62 mph<br>

  Autocross Event: 61.066s (34th place)<br>
  Endurance Event: Completed<br>
